Info

Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut is one of the brightest players on the professional Counter-Strike scene. Born on November 9, 2000, in France, he began his career at just 16 years old. Mathieu Herbaut has consistently displayed exceptional skills and quickly gained worldwide recognition. In his early years, experts were already predicting a brilliant future for him in CS.

In 2018, ZywOo joined the French club Vitality, and since then, he has become a key player for the team. The success of the lineup largely depends on his individual performance. ZywOo stats are simply incredible — in his first year playing at a tier-1 level, he was recognized by the community as the best player of the year, overtaking s1mple. At the age of 23, Mathieu has already become a Major champion, won numerous other tournaments, and earned over a million dollars in prize money. He has also frequently been named the best player of various events. ZywOo's performances and achievements have made him not only a fan favorite but also one of the highest-paid players in CS. Exact salary figures have not been disclosed, but it is rumored to be over $100k.

ZywOo age only highlights his talent, as he has achieved remarkable success before turning 23 — an age when many players are just beginning to break into the top ranks of professional CS. His main goals are victories at major tournaments and Majors. ZywOo is steadily working toward becoming the best player in CS2.
